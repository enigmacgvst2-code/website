Data Release & Access

Data Products

Our processed data products, including nightly reduced single exposures data, nightly mosaics, difference imaging, and mosaic stacks, will be systematically made available through the ADHOC data center.

The data center overview can be found at:
https://www.fisica.unina.it/strutture/adhoc

Access Requirements

Data access is granted to researchers with LSST data rights. To request access to the data products, please send an email to either Marianna Annunziatella or Claudio Grillo, and complete the dedicated Google Form provided on the ADHOC website.

Note: All public ancillary data corresponding to the targets are linked directly within their respective target pages on this website.
